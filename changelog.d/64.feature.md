PyTES registers the references it is built on, so `pybamm.print_citations()` lists them:
- the models: Luckins and Brosa Planella (2025) for the finned-tube models; Brosa Planella (2025) for the packed bed, plus Ismail and Henríquez (2002) when the capsules are resolved along the bed and across each capsule;
- the heat transfer correlations and corrections when a model uses them: Gnielinski (2013), Wakao, Kaguei and Funazkri (1979) and Jeffreson (1972). A correlation registered with `pytes.submodels.register_correlation` can list its own references in `citations`;
- Schumann (1929), when `pytes.validation.schumann` is used.
