Every model's results change: the fluid is advected at second order by default, and the default meshes are coarser (sharp front 20 by 20 instead of 40 by 40, packed bed 40 by 40 instead of 40 by 80, or 40 instead of 80 for a lumped capsule), for smaller errors in less time. To keep earlier results, pass `spatial_methods={**model.default_spatial_methods, "flow path": pytes.compat.fluid_spatial_method("first order")}` and the earlier `var_pts`.
