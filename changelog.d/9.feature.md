New `pytes.parameter_layers`, which composes parameter sets from layers (operation, coupling, heat transfer fluid, PCM, metal, and the family geometry as a flow path and a storage element), so that one part, such as the PCM or the fins, can be swapped with `swap` without touching the rest. It ships layers with cited sources (water, Hytherm 600, aluminium, steel, copper, the Nallusamy (2007) paraffin, A164, and the representative PCM 10N, PCM 30 and PCM 60); `operation.charge` and `operation.discharge` build an operation from a PCM, and `template` gives an empty layer to fill in. See the new example notebook `examples/parameter_layers.ipynb`.
