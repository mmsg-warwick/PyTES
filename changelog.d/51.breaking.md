Packed-bed results change: during a charge, the fluid temperature at each height no longer overshoots and then falls by 1 K to 2 K. While the PCM at a capsule's surface melted, the capsule took up to five times less heat than the heat transfer coefficient gives; it now takes exactly that heat. The default packed bed now fits the measurements of Nallusamy et al. (2007) better at every height, and the finned-tube models move by at most 0.006 K. There is nothing to change in your code.
