Flow-dependent heat transfer coefficients: the new option `"heat transfer coefficient"` computes the coefficient from the flow with a Nusselt correlation, `"Gnielinski"` in the finned-tube models and `"Wakao-Kaguei"` in the packed bed, instead of the constant `"Heat transfer coefficient [W.m-2.K-1]"` (still the default). Every model now outputs `"Heat transfer coefficient [W.m-2.K-1]"`, and with a correlation also the surface Reynolds, Prandtl and Nusselt numbers. Correlations of your own, a function `nusselt(Re, Pr)`, become option values with `pytes.submodels.register_correlation`, and `pytes.conformance.check_model` checks a model that uses one. See the new example notebook `examples/heat_transfer_coefficient.ipynb`.
