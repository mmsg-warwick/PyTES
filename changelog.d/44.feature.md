PyTES plot colours, chosen to stay readable with colour blindness and led by the brand orange: the colour cycles PyTES Thermal and PyTES Thermal (dark) as matplotlib styles (`plt.style.use("pytes.thermal")`, `"pytes.thermal_dark"`) and as `pytes.plotting.THERMAL` and `THERMAL_DARK`, and the perceptually uniform colourmaps `"pytes.ember"` (sequential) and `"pytes.thaw"` (diverging), registered with matplotlib when `pytes.plotting` is imported. They are not the default yet; a new documentation page, Plot colours, explains them and the reasons behind them. The `plot` extra now needs matplotlib 3.7 or later.
