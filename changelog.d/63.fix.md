PyTES works with PyBaMM 26.11, whose upwinding now takes the inlet value itself at the inflow (pybamm-team/PyBaMM#5860): the fluid's inlet condition adapts to the installed PyBaMM, so results are the same on 26.11 as on earlier releases. Before this fix, PyTES on PyBaMM 26.11 let fluid at the wrong temperature flow in, so energy was not conserved.
