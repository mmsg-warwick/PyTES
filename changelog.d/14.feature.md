A lumped capsule for the packed bed (issue #5): `options={"storage element": "lumped"}` gives each capsule a uniform temperature, much faster and accurate when the capsule's Biot number is small, and `"lumped with Jeffreson"` also corrects the heat transfer coefficient for the conduction inside the capsule (new submodels `LumpedStorage` and `LumpedGeometry`, and the flux law `Jeffreson`).
