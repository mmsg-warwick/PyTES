A lumped capsule for the packed bed (issue #5): `options={"storage element": "lumped"}` gives each capsule a uniform temperature, much faster and accurate when the capsule's Biot number is small. `"internal resistance": "Jeffreson"` corrects the heat transfer coefficient for the conduction inside a lumped capsule. New submodels `LumpedStorage` and `LumpedGeometry`, and the flux law `Jeffreson`.
