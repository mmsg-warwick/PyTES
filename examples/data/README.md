# Example data

`Nallusamy2007_{HTF,PCM}_{0.25L,0.5L,0.75L,1L}.csv`: measured temperatures of the heat
transfer fluid and of the PCM at a quarter, half, three quarters and the full height of the
packed bed, during a charge, digitised from N. Nallusamy, S. Sampath and R. Velraj (2007),
"Experimental investigation on a combined sensible and latent heat storage system integrated
with constant/varying (solar) heat sources", Renewable Energy 32, 1206–1227. Copied from the
[encapsulated-LTES](https://github.com/mmsg-warwick/encapsulated-LTES) repository at commit `023bf58`.
